Agent Observability Series

Practical notes on diagnosing, debugging, and monitoring autonomous agents — what signals tell you when a session is struggling, how to trace unexpected behavior, and how to build measurement infrastructure that compounds.

#observability#debugging#autonomous-agents

Autonomous agents fail in ways that are hard to see. The session looks productive. The journal says something happened. But a signal was lost, a cursor skipped rows, or five consecutive sessions dismissed the same red check without connecting them. This series collects concrete notes on building the instrumentation to find those failures: trajectory metrics, flight recorders, source-grounded debugging, and the patterns that let you distinguish a real problem from an artifact of how you're measuring.
